## Heart Disease

Predicting Heart Disease Using Clinical Data (Cleveland Dataset)

In this project, the **CRISP-DM (Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining)** methodology has been used.



### 1.Business Understanding

Goal: To develop a predictive model that can accurately determine whether a patient is likely to have heart disease, based on 13 clinical attributes (such as age, sex, chest pain type, blood pressure, etc.).


Business Problem:
Cardiovascular diseases are one of the leading causes of death globally. Early diagnosis of heart disease can significantly reduce mortality and treatment costs. However, relying solely on manual analysis by doctors can be time-consuming and prone to human error.

The goal of this project is to build a machine learning model that can support healthcare professionals in the early diagnosis of heart disease. The model will classify patients as:

0 → No heart disease (Healthy)

1 → Presence of heart disease (Unhealthy)

This binary classification task helps in clinical decision-making, resource allocation, and prioritizing patients for further tests or treatment.
